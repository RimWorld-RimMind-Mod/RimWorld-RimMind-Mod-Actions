[h1]RimMind - Actions (自主行动)[/h1]

[i]让 AI 真正操控你的殖民者——不只是聊天，更是合乎人性的自主行动。[/i]
[i]Empower AI to truly direct your colonists — Not just dialogue, but autonomous and organic in-game actions.[/i]

[hr]
[h2]📖 中文介绍 / Chinese Overview[/h2]

[b]RimMind-Actions[/b] 是 RimMind 智能体套件的“执行器官”。在原版 RimWorld 中，殖民者只是遵循死板工作优先级的打工机器。而 RimMind-Actions 将底层原子工具组合为高级复合机制（Composite ToolCalls），让殖民者在面临疲惫、饥饿、伤病或突发袭击时，能够自主做出合乎常理的求生与日常决策。

[h3]🌟 核心特性[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]高级复合动作 (Composite Tools)[/b]：
  [list]
  [*] [b]紧急回避 (flee_emergency)[/b]：突遭袭击、大火或致命危险时，小人本能避开危险区域逃往安全庇护所。
  [*] [b]进食与娱乐 (eat_and_recreation)[/b]：身心俱疲时，智能寻觅最佳餐食与娱乐设施，防止心情雪崩。
  [*] [b]战地分诊与急救 (triage_patient)[/b]：医生自动识别失血与濒死同伴，实施最高优先级的急救与护理。
  [*] [b]静养与体能恢复 (stabilize_rest)[/b]：在筋疲力尽前主动躺卧休养。
  [/list]
[*] [b]4 级风险安全门禁 (Risk Gating)[/b]：每个动作具备 Low / Medium / High / Critical 风险评级。你可以一键开启高危拦截审批，杜绝 AI 越权做出意外破坏性举动。
[*] [b]无缝衔接 Advisor 与 Core[/b]：动作执行结果会产生生理与心理反馈（饱腹感、娱乐、精力），形成完整的“感知-决策-执行-反馈”闭环。
[/list]

[hr]
[h2]🌐 English Overview[/h2]

[b]RimMind-Actions[/b] acts as the "hands and feet" of the RimMind AI suite. Instead of passively following mechanical work priorities, pawns utilize Composite ToolCalls to execute lifelike survival and lifestyle actions when prompted by their autonomous agent loops or the AI Advisor.

[h3]🌟 Key Highlights[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Smart Composite ToolCalls[/b]:
  [list]
  [*] [b]Emergency Flee[/b]: Pawns instinctively retreat from active fire, raids, or fatal perils to designated safe spots.
  [*] [b]Eat & Recreation[/b]: Colonists proactively seek out meals and social activities before mental breaks occur.
  [*] [b]Triage Patient[/b]: Medically skilled pawns automatically prioritize stabilizing bleeding companions.
  [*] [b]Rest & Stabilize[/b]: Pawns rest and recover before exhaustion causes catastrophic collapses.
  [/list]
[*] [b]Four-Tier Risk Safety Gates[/b]: Actions are categorized into Low, Medium, High, and Critical risk. Dangerous decisions can require player approval through interactive dialogs.
[*] [b]Complete Agent Feedback Loop[/b]: Action results provide direct physiological feedback (Joy, Rest, Food), completing the cognitive loop.
[/list]
